# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Oshioki a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes using welcoming and inclusive language, being
respectful of differing viewpoints, giving and gracefully accepting
constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing
empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or
private harassment, publishing others' private information without explicit
permission, and any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers
via GitHub private message or the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Complaints will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
